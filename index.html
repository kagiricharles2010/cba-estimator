Scope and disclaimer

Demo, not certified. This tool is unofficial and has not been checked or endorsed by UASU, IPUCCF, FKE, the Ministry of Education, KRA or any university. The figures are estimates, not pay advice. Your payroll office and UASU chapter have the final word.

Included: basic salary (Schedule I); commuter and professorial allowances (Schedule II); arrears from Jul 2025 until new salaries start; PAYE, SHIF, Housing Levy, NSSF and pension; union dues at 2% of basic, including the basic part of arrears. Pension, NSSF, SHIF and Housing Levy are deducted before PAYE.

Not included:

  • Leave and Book & ICT allowances.
  • Passage and baggage allowance (no rates given).
  • Items still under negotiation (due 28 Feb 2027).
  • Restored strike-period pay (Sept–Oct 2026).
  • SACCO, loans, HELB and other voluntary deductions.
  • Gratuity for contract staff (set pension to 0% if you have none).
  • Effects on pension or retirement benefits for staff who have left.

Assumed:

  • House allowance is the standard rate for your grade and isn't changed by the agreement.
  • Increments continue each year in the month you choose.
  • Promotions start at the grade and notch you enter.
  • Arrears are taxed in the month they're paid.
  • Universities paying above negotiated rates keep their rates.

Source: Return to Work Formula, IPUCCF (FKE) and UASU, 30 Oct 2026. Schedules I and II. Where your copy differs, your copy is right.
