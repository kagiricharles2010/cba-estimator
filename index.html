Scope and disclaimer

Demo, not certified. This tool is unofficial and has not been checked or endorsed by UASU, IPUCCF, FKE, the Ministry of Education, KRA or any university. The figures are estimates, not pay advice. Your payroll office and UASU chapter have the final word.

Included: basic salary (Schedule I); commuter and professorial allowances (Schedule II); arrears from Jul 2025 to Dec 2026; PAYE, SHIF, Housing Levy, NSSF and pension; union dues at 2% of basic and of arrears.

Not included:

  • Leave and Book & ICT allowances.
  • Passage and baggage allowance (no rates given).
  • Items still under negotiation (due 28 Feb 2027).
  • Restored strike-period pay (Sept–Oct 2026).
  • SACCO, loans, HELB and other voluntary deductions.

Assumed: house allowance doesn't change; increments continue each year in the month you choose; promotions start at the grade and notch you enter; arrears are taxed in the month they're paid. KRA lets you spread arrears tax back over earlier years, which can lower it. Universities already paying above these rates keep their rates.

Source: Return to Work Formula, IPUCCF (FKE) and UASU, 30 Oct 2026. Clauses 8–12, Schedules I and II. Where your copy differs, your copy is right.
